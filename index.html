How it works

Money is given away all the time. It just isn't advertised anywhere you'd look. We do the looking.

  1. We searchEvery week we go through funders, universities, companies and local programs for open applications.
  2. We filterAnything with a fee, a dead link, or an unclear winner is thrown out. What's left is short and real.
  3. You get one emailMonday morning. Each entry says what it pays, who qualifies, the deadline and how long it takes to apply.
  4. You applyClick through to the official page. You never pay us anything to apply, and neither do they.
